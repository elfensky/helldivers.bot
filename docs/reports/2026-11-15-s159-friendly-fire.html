The S159 friendly-fire spike: cause and fix

helldivers.bot · evidence of 2026-11-15 · fix in elfensky/helldivers.bot#677

Technical summary

The S159 Illuminate counters in h1_statistic hold one corrupt step. It came from the official HD1 API and was stored unchanged. It is not an integer overflow. The data migration 20261115120000_fix_s159_illuminate_statistics subtracts the step. S159 then reads 0.55% friendly fire.

In plain words

Think of a car's mileage counter. An overflow is the counter rolling past 999,999 back to 0. Here, one 15-minute trip added a billion kilometres. The trip itself was normal: 17 missions and 73 deaths. The counter is big enough. One trip report was false, and the fix removes that one trip.

The corrupt step

In the bucket of 2026-07-20 08:00 UTC, four S159 Illuminate counters jumped. Missions and deaths stayed normal. The last column is the season's own per-mission rate, applied to the step's 17 missions.

At the end of the season the row could not be real:

  • Accidentals (1,862,326,958) were about 6,550 times the deaths (284,289). An accidental is a death, and in other rows accidentals are about 20–26% of deaths.
  • Kills (106,504,454) were higher than hits (33,583,085). In other rows, kills are 20–50% of hits.

Why it is not an integer overflow

  • JavaScript numbers hold integers exactly up to 253 − 1 (about 9 × 1015). The largest value is about 4.8 million times below that limit. A precision loss rounds the last digits; it does not add 1.86 billion.
  • The five counter columns have been bigint since migration 20250722163100_bigint. The value is also below the old int32 limit (2,147,483,647).
  • An overflow sends one counter negative, or close to 4.29 billion. Here four counters rose together and stayed high.

Where the values came from

  • The bot stores the official API's 11 statistics fields unchanged, and does no arithmetic on them.
  • Two databases hold the same final values: production, and a local development database. The local one wrote its final S159 row at a later poll time (1784674885, production 1784674801), so it most likely polled on its own.
  • The official API keeps no statistics history, so the original report cannot be read again.
  • The step looks like one false mission report that the game server added to its totals. This cause is unconfirmed.

The fix

  • Data migration. It subtracts the step from every S159 Illuminate row that carries it. The guard is accidentals >= 1862270850; rows before the jump hold fewer than 60,000. A database without the corrupt rows does not change. Once fixed, the Illuminate row reads 0.73% friendly fire and 43.6% accuracy, close to S160 Illuminate (0.71% and 43.5%).
  • Ingest warning. The poller adds a statistics.implausible warning when a faction has more accidentals than deaths. The route logs it and reports it to GlitchTip at warning level. The row is still stored.
  • Also corrected: the S159 archive's death tooltip ("1.9B accidental / 1.5M total deaths"), its "By the numbers" line, and the all-time Shots per Planet figure.
  • Rollout. Staging applies the migration on its first start after the merge into develop. Production applies it at the next release.

Limits

  • The step's real share goes with it: about 0.02% of each S159 Illuminate total.
  • A corrupt counter stays corrupt until the season ends, so the warning repeats on every poll. GlitchTip groups the repeats into one issue.

Sources: /stats and /archives?season=159 payloads on helldivers.bot, the official HD1 API (get_campaign_status, get_snapshots), and a read-only query of the S159 Illuminate series (3,388 rows).
