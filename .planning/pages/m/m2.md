# The rebuilt dashboard works on every screen

## What you get

The relaunched site makes the live war dashboard its homepage and works on phone, tablet and desktop, with notifications and accounts.

## Where it stands

Shipped 2026-04-08: 4 phases done.

## Done means

- 0.5. Relaunch — The homepage becomes the live war dashboard, on a rewritten schema with seeded seasons, an API-key gate and war outcomes (0.10.0–0.14.0; GitHub Phases 1–4) (completed 2026-03-27)
- 0.6. Responsive redesign — Design tokens and a mobile-first layout scale the dashboard to tablet and desktop (0.15.0–0.22.3; GitHub Phases 5–7; #167, #168, #186) (completed 2026-04-04)
- 0.7. Live updates and notifications — Toasts, web push and the PWA ship; live data arrives by SSE in 0.24.0 and by 10 s polling from 0.27.0 (GitHub Phase 8; #24, #41, #229) (completed 2026-04-08)
- 0.8. Accounts — Players sign in through BetterAuth and get a profile page, and admins get a panel (0.25.0–0.26.0; #198, #248) (completed 2026-04-08)
