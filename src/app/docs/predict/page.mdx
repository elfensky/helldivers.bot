## Two kinds of prediction

Everything on this page falls into one of two boxes, and the site never
mixes them up — one is **mechanical** (a game rule we measured, worth a
real countdown), the other requires **actual prediction** (a model judged
against a pre-registered accuracy bar, worth a band at best):

| | 🔩 Mechanical (clocks) | 🎲 Statistical (models) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What | Assault trigger at full points · 48h assault timeout · counteroffensive fire · defend chain · assault gate | Assault-start ETA (pace) · free-wave timing · which faction is next |
| Typical error | **~0 — minutes** (48h timeout: 544/544 exact; counterattack within 10 min: 467/474; backtested 0.0h vs the statistical model's 9.2h during assaults) | Assault ETA: **passed the bar** (3–4× better than naive, ships as an ETA). Free waves: **±8h typical** on a ~44h cycle — five modeling attempts, bar never met, ships as a band |
| Honest caveat | The counteroffensive is conditional: it fires only if the assault fails (~59% of the time — the pace verdict tells you which way it's leaning) | A band is a band: "~20h (14-32h) · 63% within 24h", calibrated to hold at those rates, never a countdown |

## ⚔️ Attacks: arithmetic, not fortune-telling

A homeworld assault is not random. It fires **the moment a faction's
campaign bar fills** — the instant the last of its 10 sectors completes.
We've checked this against every attack across ~160 recorded wars: it's a
hard trigger, not a tendency.

That turns "when is the next assault?" into a school problem:

> **time left = points still needed ÷ how fast points are moving**

The dashboard measures the pace over the last 24 hours, corrects for how old
the latest reading is and for the weekly rhythm (weekends run faster), and
shows the answer as a window rather than a single number — asymmetric on
purpose, because arrival = work ÷ pace, and a slowdown delays the arrival
more than an equal speedup hastens it. Backtested war by war, this forecast
passed the project's pre-registered accuracy bar outright, which is why it
gets to be a real ETA on the dashboard.

For an assault that's already running, the event cards add a **completion
verdict** (▲ on track / ▼ behind): at the average pace so far, does the bar
fill before the 48-hour deadline?

## 🛡️ Defends: three mechanics and one dice roll

Defend waves _look_ random. Most of the pattern turns out to be machinery,
measured across 5,000+ recorded defends:

1. **The chain.** Lose a defend and the next one starts within minutes
   (~97% of the time). Win, and the "train" ends. Everything inside a train
   is mechanical — only the _first_ wave of a train needs forecasting.
2. **The counterattack clock.** A failed homeworld assault always runs
   exactly **48 hours**, and the moment it times out a counterattack lands
   on that same faction — within minutes, every time in recorded history.
   While an assault is running, the dashboard shows this as a real
   countdown: _"if the assault fails, counterattack at ⟨time⟩."_ That line
   is a mechanic, not a model.
3. **The gate.** No random defend ever starts while an assault is running —
   the hidden invasion timer is paused. Win the assault and the held timer
   releases: the next wave often lands within minutes of the victory.
4. **The dice roll.** Strip all of that away and what's left is one global
   hidden timer: after a train ends, the next free wave arrives ~32 hours
   later on average, with genuine randomness no model has beaten — five
   modeling attempts, each judged against a pre-registered bar, each miss
   recorded on the Defends page.

So the dashboard's **Predicted Wave card** runs two separate forecasts,
because these are two different problems. **No assault running:** it looks
up every moment in ~160 wars that looked like this one and shows the median
with the middle-50% window around it — _"~20h (14-32h) · 63% within 24h"_ —
a calibrated band, never a fake countdown. **Assault running:** the dice are
paused (mechanic #3), so the band steps aside and the card shows mechanic
#2's deterministic clock instead — _"counterattack ⟨time⟩"_ — phrased
through the assault's live pace: _"behind pace"_ means expect it, _"on pace
to succeed"_ means only if the players falter.

## The rule book

Every rule below is confirmed against the full recorded history (the exact
counts, and the committed script that re-verifies each one, are on the
detail pages):

1. **Two defends never run at the same time.** Zero overlaps across 120,000+
   defend pairs — there is a single global "invasion slot."
2. **A faction never defends while you're assaulting its homeworld.**
   Cross-faction overlap is fine (a defend and a *different* faction's
   assault co-run routinely).
3. **At most three events run at once** — up to three simultaneous assaults,
   or two assaults plus one defend. Never two defends.
4. **The chain:** a lost defend is followed by another within ~10 minutes
   (~97%); a won defend ends the train.
5. **Assaults trigger deterministically** the instant a faction's campaign
   bar fills (9 of 10 sectors is therefore the "assault pending" window,
   and defend waves go quiet through it).
6. **Losses run out the clock; wins end early.** Failed assaults last exactly
   48 hours — a timeout, 544 of 544 in history — and failed defends run their
   full timer too, 3,179 of 3,260 at exactly 150 minutes. Wins are the
   opposite: they end the moment the points fill (1,681 of 1,833 won defends
   finish inside the timer; won assaults at a median 37h). So while an event
   is live, its countdown is the **loss** time and its fill ETA is the **win**
   time — which is why the event cards name the outcome instead of showing a
   bare ETA.
7. **A failed assault is answered immediately:** the counterattack lands on
   that faction within minutes of the timeout, almost always on region 9 —
   and counterattack trains are harder to stop than normal ones (players
   win the first defend ~23% vs ~47%).
8. **The invasion timer is gated during assaults:** no random defend has
   ever started while an assault was running.
9. **Victory releases the held timer:** after a won assault, the next wave
   often lands within minutes — the timer had already fired and was waiting
   for the gate to open.
10. **The free waves are genuinely random:** one global timer, ~32h average
    delay (gamma-shaped), faction drawn at spawn — mostly evenly, with a
    ~61% tilt toward a faction sitting one sector from its assault.

## What would make these better

Data, not cleverness. The open improvements are tracked as issues and
blocked on history accumulating: outcome-conditioning the counterattack
forecast needs ~30+ assaults with minute-level progress capture
([#487](https://github.com/elfensky/helldivers.bot/issues/487)), and the
attack-side refinements wait on more high-resolution seasons
([#481](https://github.com/elfensky/helldivers.bot/issues/481),
[#477](https://github.com/elfensky/helldivers.bot/issues/477),
[#484](https://github.com/elfensky/helldivers.bot/issues/484)).
